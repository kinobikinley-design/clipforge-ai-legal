Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ClipForge AI handles information when you use the application.

2. Information Processed

Depending on the features used, ClipForge AI may process video files, audio files, transcripts, generated clips, captions, hashtags and application settings.

3. Local Processing

ClipForge AI is designed as a local-first desktop application. Video files and project data are processed and stored on your computer unless you choose to use a third-party service or upload content to an external platform.

4. TikTok Integration

If you connect your TikTok account, ClipForge AI uses TikTok's authorisation process and the permissions you approve. Basic profile information may be accessed to identify your connected account.

When you choose to upload a video, the selected video is transferred to TikTok as a draft. TikTok processes that content under its own policies. ClipForge AI does not automatically publish videos to your profile.

5. Third-Party Services

Optional features may use third-party services for AI processing, speech recognition or social media integration. Those services have their own privacy policies.

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared when necessary to provide a feature you choose or when required by law.

7. Data Retention

Files stored locally remain on your device until you delete them. You can disconnect your TikTok account and revoke application permissions through TikTok's settings.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by ClipForge AI. No digital storage or transmission method is completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

ClipForge AI is not intended for children under 13 or the minimum age required by applicable law.

10. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated periodically. Updated versions will be published on this page.
